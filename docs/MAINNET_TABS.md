# Historical mainnet profile and wallet-tabs evidence

> This document retains the earlier mainnet/table/tab campaigns and their original encrypted-backup policy. That policy is superseded: current setup records and confirms the BIP39 phrase in Go, and recovery imports the phrase into an empty profile. The app has no encrypted-file backup creation, verification or restore workflow. All passing counts and artifact statements below apply to the historical sources/runtimes described, not the current rebuild. See [PHRASE_RECOVERY.md](PHRASE_RECOVERY.md), [current layout](WALLET_LAYOUT.md) and [current frontend boundaries](FRONTEND_SIGNER_TESTS.md) for new behavior and evidence.


The complete desktop now starts in mainnet mode. Its Go coordinator and signer use a separate mainnet profile; existing testnet ciphertext is preserved and remains accessible with `./cryptowallet --profile=testnet`. No profile is copied, reclassified or automatically initialized with a new root. Wallet creation/import/restore still requires an explicit action and native credential input.

Each deterministic account group has a top tab, starting with Main account. Its table contains every configured network wallet: nine mainnet networks and eleven assets across all six adapter families. Rows show the network, assets, native-currency balance and a one-line address with ellipsis/full tooltip and a copy icon. Clicking a row or its network name opens that wallet; Enter or Space activates a focused row. Copying leaves the overview open and passes only the captured account ID to Go, which independently derives and displays the authoritative address before copying it.

Opening a wallet creates or selects its tab. Its page shows that wallet's selected-asset balance, native gas balance where relevant, Send/Receive controls and provider configuration. Scoped history and the payment journal are in Settings with independent wallet and asset selectors. Send remains hidden until clicked and opens even before backup verification; preparation remains gated. Receive shows a backup guide when required and continues to its authoritative QR only after explicit successful verification in the unchanged wallet/session. Closing Send or the active wallet clears visible payment intent and invalidates delayed preparation/scan replies, while durable reservations remain in the journal. Middle-click or Delete closes an opened wallet tab; deterministic account tabs remain available. Closing an inactive tab preserves the active wallet's form, focus and pending work. Balance formatting uses integer strings and registry decimals, including values beyond JavaScript Number precision. A missing or failed result remains unknown. The journal filters account ID, network and asset; navigation never cancels durable reservations, retries a payment or sends automatically. Late responses cannot populate another wallet's tab. Lock clears all tabs and cached public data.

Receive opens the authoritative native window with the full network/address, locally generated QR and Copy address button. Copy uses the signer's independently derived address, stays inside that window and gives feedback; Done alone does not copy. A deliberate public copy remains on the clipboard if the user subsequently cancels. The scrollable body keeps action buttons available on the tested small display. The displayed QR is resized locally; its original PNG remains unchanged for saving and verification. Electron no longer embeds a second receive QR. A successful Decred receive still updates its committed descriptor after its tab closes, but a result from before lock is discarded.

The former left Wallet menu is under the top-right Settings button. Its modal panel retains setup, backup, password and account-recovery actions, traps focus, closes with Escape or a backdrop click and clears on lock. NEAR/Aptos association has a separate public address field in network details, so it remains available without opening Send.

Mainnet receive/copy and signing retain verified-backup requirements and native authorization. Mainnet connectivity is separate from production acceptance: this change does not complete the funded-network, installed-OS, 24-hour or independent-review gates.
